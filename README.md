## About the Project
**1. Enforces a Hard Carbon & Cost Cap**
Voluntary dashboards fail when evening electricity turns up to 3x dirtier and 20% to 50% more expensive than midday solar. This hardware controller acts like a strict mobile data cap for pollution and power spend.

**2. Rations Power Like a Traffic Cop**
Tracking live grid pollution and wattage locally, it snaps off non-essential gadgets (100% power and cost cut when idle), throttles heavy computer work by 30% to 50%, and keeps critical equipment running.

**3. Learns Your Priorities in Real Time**
Pressing a physical override button during an urgent task forces the chip to adapt on the spot—protecting priority work while cutting secondary devices harder to hold the exact same carbon and cost ceiling.

**4. Freezes Learning on Broken Sensors**
Built-in fault detection flags loose wires or malfunctioning sensors immediately, freezing adaptation so corrupted readings never trigger erratic power cuts.
